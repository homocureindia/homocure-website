What Is Homeopathy?

A brief look at the practice our formulations are built around.

The Principle of "Like Cures Like"

Homeopathy is a system of alternative medicine conceived in 1796 by the German physician Samuel Hahnemann. It rests on the principle of "similia similibus curentur" — like cures like — the idea that a substance capable of producing certain symptoms in a healthy person could, in a highly diluted form, be used to address similar symptoms in someone who is unwell.

Homeopathic remedies are prepared through a process called potentization: a raw ingredient is first extracted into a concentrated mother tincture, which is then serially diluted and vigorously shaken (succussion) to create different potency levels — commonly labelled 30C, 200C or 1M depending on how many rounds of dilution are performed.

In India, homeopathy is recognised as one of the country's official systems of medicine and is regulated by the Ministry of AYUSH under the Drugs & Cosmetics Act, with manufacturing standards laid out in Schedule T of the Drugs Rules.
