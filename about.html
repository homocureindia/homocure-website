A Brief History

The Journey of Homeopathy

Understanding where the practice behind our formulations comes from.

1796 — Germany

Samuel Hahnemann Founds Homeopathy

While translating a medical text on cinchona bark, physician Samuel Hahnemann tests the substance on himself and notices it produces malaria-like symptoms. He concludes that a substance capable of causing symptoms in a healthy person could, in diluted form, treat similar symptoms in someone unwell — the principle of "like cures like."

1830s — Europe

Homeopathy Spreads and Standardizes

Homeopathy spreads rapidly across Europe. American homeopath Constantine Hering introduces the centesimal (C) potency scale still used today, and the first dedicated homeopathic hospital opens in Leipzig in 1832.

1975 — India

National Quality Standards Introduced

The Homoeopathic Pharmacopoeia Laboratory is established in Ghaziabad to lay down national standards for the identity, purity and quality of homeopathic medicines manufactured in India.

2014 — India

Ministry of AYUSH Formed

The Government of India establishes the Ministry of AYUSH to regulate Ayurveda, Yoga, Unani, Siddha and Homeopathy as recognised systems of medicine under the Drugs & Cosmetics Act, 1940.

2026 — Noida

Homocure Pharmaceuticals Prepares to Launch

Building on this long-established practice, Homocure is developing a product line of mother tinctures, dilutions, biochemic tablets, syrups, drops and ointments — ahead of its upcoming launch.
